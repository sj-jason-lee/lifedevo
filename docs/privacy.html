Pasture

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 8, 2026

Pasture ("we," "our," or "the app") is a Bible devotional application designed to support your spiritual growth through daily readings, devotionals, and reflections. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we collect.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Pasture, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information — email address and display name, provided during registration.
  • Church membership — your selected church or community affiliation within the app.
  • Devotional reading history — records of which devotionals you have read.
  • Reflections — personal reflections or notes you write in response to devotionals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Pasture app and its features.
  • Personalize your experience, such as tracking your reading progress.
  • Send push notifications (with your permission) for daily devotionals.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app stability.

3. Third-Party Services

Pasture uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting. Your account data and content are stored securely on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Sentry — error tracking and performance monitoring. Sentry may receive anonymized diagnostic data when errors occur. Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Expo — push notification delivery. Expo processes device tokens to deliver notifications. Expo Privacy Policy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use third-party advertising in the app. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, solely for the purpose of operating the app.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the app. Upon deletion, all of your personal data — including your email, display name, reading history, and reflections — will be permanently erased.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and secure authentication through Supabase, to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Pasture is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
